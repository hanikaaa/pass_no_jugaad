==================================================
1. WHAT IS PASS NO JUGAAD?
==================================================

Pass No Jugaad is an Ahmedabad-focused Navratri discovery, demand and pass-request platform.

It is NOT a conventional ticketing website.

The core product is:

PEOPLE →
tell us what kind of Navratri event/pass they want

PASS NO JUGAAD →
collects and understands this demand

ORGANISERS →
can list their events and understand what people are actively looking for

JUGAAD DROPS →
when an organiser-approved deal, allocation, referral opportunity or special offer is available, it can be featured on the platform.

The website should feel like:

"Ahmedabad's Navratri command centre."

It should NOT feel like:

"another generic ticket-booking website."

==================================================
2. PRIMARY GOALS
==================================================

There are two main audiences.

BUYERS:

1. Discover Navratri events
2. Search for events/artists/venues
3. Browse events by date
4. Filter events
5. See event details
6. Submit a pass request
7. Tell us their preferences
8. Track their requests
9. Discover Jugaad Drops
10. Join WhatsApp/Instagram updates

ORGANISERS:

1. Understand what Pass No Jugaad does
2. Submit their event
3. Provide event details
4. Provide expected crowd/capacity
5. Provide pricing and inventory information
6. Tell us what they need
7. Submit their Instagram/official event link
8. Contact Pass No Jugaad through Instagram/WhatsApp
9. Eventually receive demand insights

==================================================
3. IMPORTANT MVP RULE
==================================================

This website must be achievable as a lightweight MVP.

DO NOT build:

- complex ticketing backend
- payment gateway
- ticket wallet
- QR ticket generation
- complicated authentication
- complex inventory management
- full marketplace infrastructure
- unnecessary user accounts

Instead:

Use forms and lightweight integrations.

Buyer forms can eventually connect to:
Tally / Google Forms / Airtable / Google Sheets / email / WhatsApp.

Organiser forms can connect to:
Email + Google Sheets/Airtable + WhatsApp/Instagram.

The website should LOOK like a sophisticated product while the backend can initially be manually managed.
